Detector controls

Detectors are RemoteModule execute actions with event subscriptions, not telehealth commands. Enabling a detector subscribes to its events so detections surface as events, not as speech input. A detector stays active only until one item of its type is detected; re-enable to keep detecting. Payloads are built here; live robot delivery via the remote_chat response path is unverified and is not claimed.
